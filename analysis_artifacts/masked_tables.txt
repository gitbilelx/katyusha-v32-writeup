1. WHAT THE "MASKED TABLES" ARE (final answer)

A single PowerBASIC static-string-literal pool: 104 bytes (52 UTF-16LE words) at
.data:0x620164..0x6201cb, stored XOR-masked with 0x4e ('N') and unmasked ONCE at
process start, before any other statement mechanic runs.

   WinMain prologue (disasm of FUN_00695000, first emitted statement, marker 0x83
   written immediately after at 0x0069512c):

     0x0069511b  mov edi, 0x620164      ; pool base
     0x00695120  mov ecx, 0x68          ; 104 bytes
     0x00695125  xor byte ptr [edi], 0x4e   ; <=== the mask
     0x00695128  inc edi
     0x00695129  dec ecx
     0x0069512a  jne 0x695125

   Gate-condition proof: the ONLY `xor ..., 0x4e` site in the entire image is
   0x00695125 (capstone scan of all executable sections). Bytes at rest are NOT
   valid UTF-16 in memory until this loop runs; every pool read in decompiled
   code is downstream of it.

4. REJECTED HYPOTHESES (record per tasks.txt T4)

   (a) "XOR-only tail decrypt, len 0x4e" — REJECTED. 0x4e is never a payload
       length; it is the single-byte mask. No F4-cipher structure in the pool
       (no keys, no sbox math, no ROR).
   (b) "'N'-filler literal padding" — REJECTED. The 'N' bytes (0x4e) de-mask to
       0x00; they are the UTF-16LE high/zero bytes of ordinary wide literals,
       not padding characters.
   (c) "fixed-protocol offset shift +0xb stride" — RESOLVED, not a stride:
       Ghidra auto-named the anchored symbol s_..._00620189 mid-pool; each of
       the 9 separately-referenced literals is cited at its own offset. The
       most common offset (+0xb from 0x620189 = 0x620194) simply selects the
       CRLF literal (120 refs).
